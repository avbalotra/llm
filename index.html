SECTION 02 // MACRO ARCHITECTURE

One sentence. Six stages. A whole world of computation.

Modern Large Language Models do not read words like humans do. Instead, text passes through six deterministic mathematical transformations—from raw characters to high-dimensional geometry and probability distributions.

WHAT HAPPENS AT THIS STAGE

Raw human language enters the model as an arbitrary UTF-8 encoded text stream. At this initial phase, the model possesses no concept of grammar, semantics, or meaning—only a contiguous sequence of characters and byte sequences.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SYSTEM DESIGN

Neural networks are mathematical functions that only operate on numerical tensors. Natural language must be methodically structured, validated, and normalized before any downstream algebraic operations can commence.

SECTION 05 // MULTI-HEAD ATTENTION

How does an LLM understand context?

Words possess no static, invariant meaning. In the sentence "The animal didn't cross the road because it was tired", what does "it" refer to? Self-attention allows tokens to dynamically attend to every other token, capturing both grammatical syntax and coreference semantics simultaneously.

SECTION 06 // PROBABILITY & GENERATION

How does an LLM decide what comes next?

At the final layer, the contextualized representation is projected into a vector of unnormalized scores (logits) across the entire vocabulary. The softmax function converts these logits into a probability distribution, which temperature scales to control entropy and creativity.
